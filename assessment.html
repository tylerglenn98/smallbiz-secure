Small Business Cybersecurity Assessment

How Secure Is Your Business?

Answer the questions below to identify possible cybersecurity risks within your business. Your answers will be used to provide a security score and recommendations.

1. Passwords & MFA

Do you use strong, unique passwords for your business accounts?

Do you use multi-factor authentication (MFA) when available?

2. Digital Payments

Is access to your payment or POS system limited to authorized employees?

3. Phishing Awareness

Do you and your employees know how to recognize suspicious emails, messages, and links?

4. Wi-Fi Security

Is your business Wi-Fi protected with a strong password?

5. Data Backups

Do you regularly back up important business information?

6. Employee Access

Do employees have their own accounts instead of sharing login information?
